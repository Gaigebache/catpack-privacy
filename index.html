CatPack Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Applies to the CatPack apps for iPhone and Android

Summary

CatPack is a lifting tracker for athletes and coaches. We collect only what the app needs to work: your account, your athlete details and the lifts and workouts you log. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from them. Apple lets you hide your real email.
  • Athlete details (optional): position, body weight and phone number.
  • Training data: the sets you log (exercise, weight, reps, date), your best lifts, workouts you log and check off, workouts you make or send, and exercises you add.
  • Teams: which teams you've joined or created, and whether you asked to be a coach.
  • Profile picture (optional): a small photo you choose.
  • Settings: your privacy choices, theme and last team picked. Some of these are kept only on your phone.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health records, advertising IDs or browsing activity.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your data in sync between your devices.
  • To show your progress charts, workout log and leaderboards.
  • To let you join teams and send workouts to teammates.

That's all. We don't use your data for advertising or sell it to anyone.

Who can see your information

  • Your teams: people on a team you join can see your name, position, body weight, best lifts and profile picture. They also see your progress chart and workout log unless you turn off Teammates can see my progress. Coaches on your team can always see your progress, and can log sets for you.
  • General leaderboard: if Show me on the General leaderboard is on, other CatPack users can see your name (or initials, if you choose), position, body weight, best lifts and photo (if allowed). You can turn this off anytime.
  • General use: lifts you log in General use are private to you.
  • Sending workouts: when you send a workout by text or email, it goes through the messaging app you pick and is shared with the people you send it to.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf and keeps it secure. Sign-in is handled by Firebase and, if you choose them, Google or Apple. Your password is never visible to us. Access to team data is limited by security rules, so only the people described above can see it.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can edit your profile, change or remove your photo, change privacy settings, and leave teams in Account & settings (tap your profile picture).
  • You can delete your account in the app: Account & settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, everything in General use, your leaderboard entries, and your own athlete profile, lifts and workout log on each team.
  • Athlete profiles a coach created for someone without an account stay with that team until the coach deletes them.

Young athletes

CatPack is made for high school and older athletes and is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
